## Positioning

**Observed:** The homepage describes connecting customer data with marketing, sales, service, and AI. Its main calls to action offer a demo and free entry. Product navigation includes marketing, sales, service, content, data, revenue, CRM, and agent tools. [S1]

**Headline:** Recorded exactly in the [source notes](raw/hubspot/2026-11-11/scrapes/source-notes.md). **Subheadline, paraphrased:** Shared customer data helps teams and AI understand customer needs. [S1]

**Inferred — high confidence:** HubSpot positions itself around platform consolidation and coordinated customer work. This describes its messaging, not independently tested product performance. [S1]
